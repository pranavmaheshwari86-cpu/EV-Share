Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Driver Cancellation Window

Drivers may cancel any confirmed booking prior to the scheduled slot start time without penalty. When cancelled, the reservation is released immediately, allowing another traveler along the corridor to claim the slot.

2. Immediate Slot Lock Release

Cancelling a booking automatically deletes the associated atomic slot lock in the database, restoring genuine real-time capacity to the marketplace.

3. Refund Processing

In this pilot demonstration, any simulated payment for a cancelled booking is instantly transitioned to a refunded status in your billing history.

4. Host Cancellation Rules

Hosts may only cancel a reservation due to documented technical power failure, maintenance, or property emergencies. Frequent cancellations without cause result in temporary suspension of the host listing.
