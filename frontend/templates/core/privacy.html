Privacy Policy

Privacy Notice & Address Disclosure Policy

1. The Host Address Protection Guarantee

We respect the privacy of residential property owners. When browsing chargers, public maps display only the approximate sector or landmark (e.g. "Near Jewar Toll Plaza, Greater Noida"). Exact house numbers, private driveway instructions, and gate codes are strictly shielded from unauthenticated users and public crawlers.

Only an authenticated driver with a confirmed and active booking can view the host's private arrival directions.

2. Information We Collect

We collect names, normalized email addresses, hashed passwords, vehicle models, and voluntary host onboarding documents (ID proof and property documents) purely for identity verification and safety auditability.

3. Zero Card Storage

EV Share never collects, handles, or stores raw payment card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs. Sandbox simulated transactions generate illustrative reference strings only.
