Safety First

Electrical Safety & Operating Standards

1. Electrical Installation Standards

All charging equipment listed on EV Share must comply with standard Indian electrical codes (CEA Standards and Central Electricity Authority Regulations for Safety and Electric Supply):

  • Dedicated branch circuit with properly rated MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker) and RCD/RCCB (Residual Current Circuit Breaker, 30mA trip sensitivity).
  • Verified grounding and earthing resistance conforming to IS 3043. Un-earthed circuits or makeshift extension cables are strictly prohibited.
  • Commercial installations must have surge protection devices (SPD) and weather-proof outdoor enclosures (IP65/IP66 rated).

2. Verification Reality vs. Equipment Certification

Important Disclaimer: While EV Share requires hosts to submit ID proof, photos, and safety declarations, an administrative approval does not replace on-site physical inspection or manufacturer certification. Drivers should always inspect cables, plugs, and charging points for visible wear, scorch marks, or water ingress before connecting their vehicle.

3. Emergency Escalation Procedure

In the event of an electrical spark, burning odor, or equipment fault:

  1. Immediately hit the emergency stop button on the wallbox if safe, or switch off the primary isolator switch.
  2. Do not forcibly yank a locked connector; press the unlock button inside your EV cabin.
  3. Evacuate the immediate area and call emergency services if fire or smoke is present.
  4. Report the incident immediately using EV Share's in-app dispute reporting tool to freeze the listing pending investigation.
