About EV Share

Bridging the Highway EV Charging Gap

Our Mission

EV Share is a peer-to-peer charging access marketplace. While public charging stations are often crowded, malfunctioning, or spaced too far apart, hundreds of hotels, restaurants, offices, and private homeowners along Indian travel corridors own underutilized, high-capacity charging points.

Our mission is to build a reliable, verified, and community-powered network that unlocks this latent capacity, giving EV drivers dependable travel confidence.

The Pilot Strategy

Rather than spreading ourselves thin with scattered chargers nationwide, our initial pilot is focused on the Delhi – Agra Yamuna Expressway corridor. Concentrating supply along this 165 km corridor allows us to test host onboarding, electrical safety compliance, booking reliability, and actual driver visits under controlled operational conditions.

Pillars of the Platform

  • Ground Truth Over Guesswork: Every listing is reviewed by administrators before being published.
  • Concurrency Safety: Atomic database locking guarantees no driver arrives to find their reserved plug occupied.
  • Privacy Protection: Exact residential addresses and gate passcodes are shielded from public maps and shown only to confirmed drivers.
