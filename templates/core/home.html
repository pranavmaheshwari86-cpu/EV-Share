Simple & Reliable

How EV Share Works

A structured peer-to-peer charging marketplace built around verified hosts and dependable reservations.

1. Discover Compatible Nodes

Filter highway corridor listings by your vehicle connector (CCS2, Type 2), charging speed, price, and schedule.

2. Instant Slot Reservation

Select an available 1-hour slot. Our atomic concurrency engine locks the slot immediately, preventing double booking.

3. Charge & Track Visit

Receive exact private directions upon confirmation. Arrive, plug in, track visit start/end, and record feedback.

Safety & Trust First

Designed with Strict Host Privacy & Rigorous Verification

Unlike unmoderated map apps, every EV Share host must submit identity proofs and property permissions reviewed by administrators before their listing can accept drivers.

  • ✓ Protected Private Addresses: Exact residential addresses and gate codes are revealed only to confirmed drivers.
  • ✓ Atomic Anti-Collision Locking: Database constraints prevent overlapping bookings on exclusive chargers.
  • ✓ Clear Telemetry Reality: Charging visits are transparently marked as manual host entries without fabricated hardware claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

The initial pilot concentrates charging nodes along the Yamuna Expressway (connecting Greater Noida to Agra). This ensures meaningful charging density for traveling EV drivers rather than scattered chargers across the country.

Hosts set a per-kWh rate. The server estimates energy consumption based on charger capacity and duration, adding an illustrative 15% platform commission and applicable GST. An immutable price snapshot is locked in at booking confirmation.

For the MVP demonstration, payments use a sandboxed simulator. Transactions reflect realistic invoices, commission splits, and refund states without collecting real card numbers.
