Operating Guide

How EV Share Operates

A clear, step-by-step walkthrough for both EV drivers and charger hosts.

For Drivers

Finding & Booking

  1. Search the Corridor: Browse the Yamuna Expressway corridor map or filter by your car's connector type (CCS2, Type 2, 15A Socket).
  2. Inspect Charger Specs: Check rated power output (e.g. 7.4 kW AC or 60 kW DC), pricing per kWh, host category, and available amenities.
  3. Select a Slot & Instant Book: Pick an open 1-hour time window. The server locks the slot atomically, preventing any conflicting reservation.
  4. Review Directions: Once confirmed, unlock the host's private arrival instructions and gate code.
  5. Charge & Rate: Arrive, record the session start/end meter readings with the host, and leave feedback.
For Hosts

Sharing & Earning

  1. Submit Verification: Complete a simple KYC application with ID proof, property ownership/access declaration, and charger details.
  2. Administrative Review: Platform administrators review your submission for safety and corridor relevance.
  3. Set Pricing & Schedule: Define your price per kWh, weekly available hours, and any blackout dates.
  4. Accept Reservations: Drivers book pre-approved slots instantly within your configured hours.
  5. Record Visit & Track Balance: Check in the driver, record meter readings upon departure, and review your earnings breakdown.
