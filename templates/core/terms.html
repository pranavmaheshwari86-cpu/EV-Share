Terms of Service — Pilot Baseline

1. The Role of EV Share

EV Share operates as an online marketplace platform connecting EV drivers with third-party charger hosts. EV Share does not own, operate, or manufacture the third-party chargers listed on the marketplace.

2. Pilot Demonstration Notice

This web application represents the MVP pilot for the Yamuna Expressway travel corridor. Financial transactions in this pilot environment are processed in simulated sandbox mode for workflow validation and unit-economics modeling.

3. Host Eligibility & Property Permissions

Hosts represent and warrant that they have legal authority, landlord consent (where applicable), and necessary permissions from housing societies or commercial property management to share electrical access on the designated premises.

4. Concurrency & Booking Policy

Confirmed reservations lock exclusive charger capacity. Both drivers and hosts agree to respect the scheduled time windows and vacate charging bays promptly upon session conclusion.
